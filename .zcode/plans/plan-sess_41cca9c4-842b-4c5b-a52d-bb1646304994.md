## Changes

### New: `src/modules/print.ts`

```ts
printWhenReady(queryClient: QueryClient): Promise<void>
```

Sequence, then `window.print()`:
1. Double `requestAnimationFrame` — lets the `isPrint` re-render commit and effect-triggered queries start before idleness is judged.
2. Poll `queryClient.isFetching()` every 150 ms until it reports **0 on two consecutive checks**, capped at 20 s (a hung request must not disable printing — keeps today's best-effort semantics as a ceiling, not the norm). Two consecutive idle checks absorb the tpopkontr zaehl-insert→`refetch()` chain that fires when a form without count rows mounts during mass print.
3. `document.fonts.ready`.
4. Every `document.images` entry not yet `complete` → `img.decode()`, each raced against a 10 s timeout, `Promise.allSettled` (broken/missing images must not block).
5. Double rAF again so recharts/`ResponsiveContainer` lays out with the final data before capture.
